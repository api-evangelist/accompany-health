# Accompany Health

> Accompany Health is a value-based healthcare company that delivers integrated in-home
> and virtual care to patients with complex, chronic conditions, primarily those covered
> by Medicaid and Medicare. Its multidisciplinary care teams combine primary care,
> behavioral health, and social care, wrapping medical treatment with support for the
> social drivers of health. The company operates in Massachusetts, Michigan (Detroit),
> and Colorado (Denver), partners with health plans and referring providers, and operates
> Accompany Medical Group, P.A. as a HIPAA Affiliated Covered Entity.

## API surface

There is none. As of 2026-08-15 the following were probed on every Accompany Health
host and all returned HTTP 404: `/openapi.json`, `/openapi.yaml`, `/swagger.json`,
`/v1/openapi.json`, `/api-docs`, `/docs`, `/redoc`, `/api`, `/graphql`, `/mcp`,
`/llms.txt`, and the whole `/.well-known/` discovery surface (`security.txt`,
`openid-configuration`, `oauth-authorization-server`, `oauth-protected-resource`,
`api-catalog`, `ai-plugin.json`, `agent-card.json`, `agent.json`). The subdomains
`api.`, `developer.` and `docs.accompanyhealth.com` do not resolve.

The only machine-readable HTTP surface on the domain is the default WordPress core
REST API at `https://accompanyhealth.com/wp-json/` — 220 routes of CMS infrastructure
that WordPress ships with every installation. It is not designed, documented or
supported by Accompany Health as a developer API and is not catalogued as one.
